# Community code of conduct

PaperWork exists to make confusing documents easier to act on. We want its community to be safe, useful, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Protect privacy. Never post another person's document, identity, or confidential information.
- State uncertainty and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Make room for people who are directly affected by the document types being discussed.
- Prefer accessible language and explain unfamiliar terms.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate misinformation, spam, and publishing confidential material are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. For now, report conduct concerns through a minimal GitHub issue that asks for private maintainer contact and contains no sensitive details.

This policy applies to the repository, discussions, community calls, and public interactions made on behalf of PaperWork.
